**Get your boilerplate seen.**

BoilerplatePress is where makers of boilerplates, starter kits and ready-made products
list their work. Submit your boilerplate once, add coupons and projects your customers
built with it, and we publish it where founders look for their next starter kit.

## What you can do

- List your boilerplate with its stack, pricing and features
- Offer coupons to new buyers
- Showcase live projects built with your boilerplate
- Share releases, updates and tutorials
